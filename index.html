Your opponent

Open a challenge and your opponent can join it from this same page.

Tap where you want your shot to go. The shot takes a moment to get there, so aim ahead of the clay (on a computer, the space bar also shoots at the crosshair). You have two barrels, and a quick second barrel kicks a little off line. The guns run from the full choke (the fastest shot, the tightest spread) to the blunderbuss (the slowest shot, an enormous spread). Clays come from the left and the right equally, in an order nobody can predict. The difficulty sets how fast they fly at first, and every level (or skeet station) is 10% faster. Points: lob 1, clay 2, swift 3, zip 4, mini 5, rabbit 5, springing teal 8, and the rare golden clay 20. In shot for shot, your opponent taps PULL to release your clays (at least 1.5 seconds apart; with two clays they may pull twice quickly), and a clay releases itself after 5 seconds. The accuracy challenge goes to whoever breaks the most; the speed challenge to the quickest average break, where a missed clay counts as 3 seconds. Every round records how many clays you broke and how quickly.
